Got this book as a gift?

Someone thinks you have a great year ahead. Here is the fastest way in:

  1. Read the Opening, The First 30 Days. It takes one evening.
  2. Start with Mission 1, The Monday Anchor, this coming Monday.
  3. Book Mission 24, The Pit Stop, a visit with your doctor, before any of the physical missions.
  4. After that, go in order or pick any mission that pulls at you. There is no wrong order.
