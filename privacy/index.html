What we collect and why

When you sign up on a book page, we collect your email address, the date of your sign-up and your confirmation. We use them to send you the bonus files you asked for, a short note when a step in a book changes, and news when a new book from Monarch Lane Press comes out. We do this because you agreed to it when you checked the box and confirmed your email.

We don't sell your email, share it with advertisers or use it for anything else.

Who handles your data

  • MailerLite stores the mailing list and sends the emails on our behalf. It records the approximate location of your sign-up and whether each email is opened, so we can tell which emails are useful.
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA) hosts this site. Like any web host, it may log visitors' IP addresses for security.

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no advertising trackers. Its fonts are hosted on this site, not loaded from a third party.

How long we keep it

We keep your email for as long as you stay on the list. If you unsubscribe, we stop emailing you and delete your address, apart from what the mailing service needs to make sure you aren't added again by mistake.

Your rights

You can unsubscribe at any time with the link at the bottom of every email. You can also ask to see, correct or delete the data we hold about you by writing to hello@monarchlanepress.com. If you live in the European Union and think we haven't handled your data properly, you can complain to your data protection authority; in France, that's the CNIL (cnil.fr).
